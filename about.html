About TELEVIDIA

Welcome to TELEVIDIA. We create films, podcasts, documentaries, live event coverage, and social content that punches!

I work primarily with two Sony FX3 cinema grade cameras, alongside an A7III as a B cam. For larger projects, I team up with other talented filmmakers, lighting experts, and sound technicians to bring everything to life.

With a background in performing arts and a deep love for filmmaking, I’ve spent my career capturing the essence of people and stories on camera. I regularly collaborate with skilled crews across the country to make every project feel filmic and authentic.

At TELEVIDIA, our goal is simple: to amplify genuine voices and tell stories that connect with people. Whether it’s a documentary, motorsport coverage, social content, or even product photography for jewellery or LEGO, we focus on creativity, unique content, and storytelling that truly resonates.
